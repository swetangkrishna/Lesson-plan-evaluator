### Lesson Plan (excerpt)

Decode AI: Real-World Applications Today

Learning Objectives
By the end of the lesson, students should be able to:
Define AI literacy in their own words and explain its importance in professional and civic contexts.
Identify and describe at least three distinct real-world use cases of AI across different industries.
Evaluate ethical and practical implications of a chosen AI use case and propose one recommendation for responsible use.
Vocabulary
Artificial Intelligence (AI) - Machines performing tasks that require human-like intelligence.
Machine Learning - Algorithms that learn patterns from data to make predictions.
Model - A mathematical or computational representation used to make predictions.
Training Data - Examples used to teach a model how to perform a task.
Supervised Learning - Learning from labeled examples to map inputs to outputs.
Unsupervised Learning - Finding patterns in unlabeled data.
Natural Language Processing (NLP) - Techniques for computers to understand human language.
Bias - Systematic error in data or models that leads to unfair outcomes.
Automation - Using technology to perform tasks without continuous human input.
