Topic: Introduction to AI Literacy and Its Use Cases 
format: 5-part lesson (objective, introduction, instruction, practice, assessment 
objective: Students will define AI literacy, identify at least three distinct use cases of AI across industries.

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
- Define AI literacy and its importance in the modern technological landscape
- Identify and explain at least three distinct use cases of AI across different industries
- Critically analyze the potential impacts and ethical considerations of AI technologies
- Develop an understanding of how AI is transforming various professional fields

Key Learning Outcomes:
1. Conceptual understanding of AI literacy
2. Industry-specific AI application knowledge
3. Critical thinking about AI's societal implications

Lecture and Discussion Components:
- Definition of AI Literacy:
  * Understanding AI technologies
  * Recognizing AI applications
  * Ethical and critical thinking about AI

- Key Concepts:
  * What is Artificial Intelligence?
  * Difference between AI, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning
  * Historical context of AI development

Interactive Element:
- Group Discussion: 'AI in Our Daily Lives'
  * Students share personal experiences with AI technologies
  * Brainstorm current AI applications they're aware of

Multimedia Presentation:
- Video clips showcasing AI innovations
- Infographics on AI's global impact

Comprehensive Overview of AI Applications Across Industries:
1. Healthcare
- Medical image diagnosis
- Predictive patient care
- Drug discovery algorithms

2. Finance
- Fraud detection systems
- Algorithmic trading
- Personalized banking recommendations

3. Education
- Adaptive learning platforms
- Personalized tutoring systems
- Administrative process optimization

4. Technology
- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
- Autonomous Systems

5. Environmental Science
- Climate modeling
- Wildlife conservation
- Renewable energy optimization

Case Study Analysis:
- Small group analysis of specific AI use case
- Presentation of findings
- Critical discussion of technological implications

Interactive Learning Activities:
1. AI Use Case Mapping Exercise
- Students create a detailed mind map of AI applications
- Identify potential future developments

2. Ethical AI Scenario Workshop
- Analyze real-world AI implementation challenges
- Discuss potential ethical dilemmas

3. Technology Demonstration
- Live demonstration of AI tools
- Platforms to explore: ChatGPT, AI image generators

Technology Requirements:
- Computers/Laptops
- Internet Connection
- AI demonstration platforms

Collaborative Learning:
- Peer review of AI use case presentations
- Group discussions on technological implications

Assessment Methods:
1. Written Assessment:
- Comprehensive quiz on AI literacy concepts
- Short essay on AI's potential societal impact

2. Presentation Evaluation:
- Rubric-based assessment of group presentations
- Depth of understanding
- Critical analysis skills

Assessment Criteria:
- Conceptual understanding (40%)
- Critical thinking (30%)
- Communication skills (20%)
- Creativity in analysis (10%)

Reflection Component:
- Individual reflection paper
- Students articulate personal insights on AI's future

Additional Evaluation Options:
- Optional advanced project
- Research paper on emerging AI technologies
